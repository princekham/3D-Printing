### Convert Text to Outlines:
- Select your text layer for "with".
- Go to Type > Create Outlines (shortcut: Shift + Ctrl + O on PC or Shift + Cmd + O on Mac).
- This turns your text into vector paths so it never loses its shape or requires the original font on other devices.
### Export as SVG:
	- Go to File > Export > Export As...
	- Choose SVG from the format dropdown menu. (I exported with 'Export Selection'. Right Click on the selection and choose 'Export Selection')
	- Click Export to open the Adobe SVG Options Dialog, choose settings like Convert to Outlines for fonts, and hit OK

### Steps to Convert JPG to SVG

- Open your image: Launch Adobe Illustrator, click File > Open, and select your JPG file.
- Select the image: Click on the image on your artboard so it is active.
- Open Image Trace: Go to the top menu and select Window > Image Trace to open the tracing panel.
- Choose a preset: In the Image Trace panel, pick a preset like Black and White Logo or High Fidelity Photo depending on your image style. Adjust the sliders if you need more or fewer details.
- Expand the trace: Go to the top menu and click Object > Image Trace > Expand (or click the Expand button on the top control bar) to turn the trace into editable vector paths.
- Export as SVG: Go to File > Export > Export As, choose SVG from the format dropdown menu, and click Export.
